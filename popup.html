How to Use

  1. Highlight text: Select any text excerpt on any webpage.
  2. Click badge or right-click: Click the floating BiasLens button or right-click and choose "Analyse with BiasLens".
  3. View analysis: Instant insights into bias score, hallucination risk, and rhetorical framing.
